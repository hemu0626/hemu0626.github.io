    Abstract

    These notes support the Chapter 3 lecture slides on multiple linear regression (MLR). They do three things that the slides state but do not derive.

    1. Complete derivations. Starting from SS(𝜷)=(𝒚−𝑿𝜷)⊤(𝒚−𝑿𝜷)SS(\bm\beta)=(\bm y-\bm X\bm\beta)^{\top}(\bm y-\bm X\bm\beta) we derive the normal equations, the unbiasedness of σ̂2=MSE\hat\sigma^2=\mathrm{MSE}, the ANOVA identity TSS=RegSS+RSS\mathrm{TSS}=\mathrm{Reg\,SS}+\mathrm{RSS}, the distribution of RegSS\mathrm{Reg\,SS} and RSS\mathrm{RSS}, their independence, and hence the exact null distribution F∼Fp,n−p−1F\sim F_{p,\,n-p-1} of the global FF-statistic. The partial FF-test and the tt-test are derived as special cases, and the identity t2=Ft^2=F is proved.

    2. A worked example small enough to do by hand. The fuel-use data of the slides (n=8n=8, p=2p=2) has a 3×33\times3 𝑿⊤𝑿\bm X^{\top}\bm X whose inverse has a rational determinant (det⁡(𝑿⊤𝑿)=3,127,073.8\det(\bm X^{\top}\bm X)=3{,}127{,}073.8). Every number in the ANOVA table, the coefficient table and the residual diagnostics is computed in full.

    3. A one-to-one check against R. For every hand-computed quantity the corresponding R command and its output are displayed, and the two are compared side by side. The companion script MTH303_Ch3_Hand_vs_R.R reproduces the whole comparison automatically and reports MATCH or DIFFERENT for each quantity.

    The intended classroom sequence is: derive on the board (§（见「ANOVA 表推导」）), compute by hand in groups (§（见「手工计算例题」）), then run the R script live and watch the two columns agree (§（见「R 验证」）–§（见「教学安排与 R 脚本」）).

    The Chapter 3 notes introduce 𝑯=𝑿(𝑿⊤𝑿)−1𝑿⊤\bm H=\bm X(\bm X^{\top}\bm X)^{-1}\bm X^{\top} and then use it. This supplement does three things.

    1. It exhibits the whole matrix. For the fuel-use example (n=8n=8, k=3k=3) all 6464 entries of 𝑯\bm H are printed, and then 𝑰n−𝑯\bm I_n-\bm H, whose diagonal gives the residual variances.

    2. It proves the properties and checks each one numerically: symmetry, idempotence, 𝑯𝑿=𝑿\bm H\bm X=\bm X, rank⁡(𝑯)=tr⁡(𝑯)=k\operatorname{rank}(\bm H)=\operatorname{tr}(\bm H)=k, eigenvalues in {0,1}\{0,1\}, and hii=∑jhij2h_{ii}=\sum_jh_{ij}^{2} (which is what bounds leverage between 1/n1/n and 11).

    3. It rebuilds every diagnostic from 𝑯\bm H alone — leverage, the full residual covariance matrix σ2(𝑰n−𝑯)\sigma^{2}(\bm I_n-\bm H), standard errors and confidence intervals, standardised and studentised residuals (both by closed form and by actually refitting the model nn times), Cook’s distance, the ANOVA table, the global and partial FF-tests, and prediction intervals. A second example with n=45n=45 and k=5k=5 shows that none of it depends on nn being small.

    The companion script MTH303_Ch3_Hat_Matrix_No_Packages.R performs all of this. It calls lm() only in blocks labelled “CHECK”, and never as the source of an answer: hatvalues(), rstandard(), rstudent(), cooks.distance(), vcov(), confint() and predict() are all re-implemented from first principles and then compared with R’s own functions.     符号与模型Notation and the model

    本节导读：先规定记法：标量形式、矩阵形式与基本假设（独立、零均值、同方差、正态）。这是后面所有推导的语言基础：学生要能默写 y = Xβ + ε，理解 k = p + 1 的含义，以及满秩条件 rank(X) = p + 1 为什么是 XᵀX 可逆的前提。

    标量形式的模型The model in scalar form

    With pp explanatory variables and nn independent observations, yi=β0+β1xi1+β2xi2+⋯+βpxip+εi,i=1,…,n,\begin{equation} \label{eq:model} y_i=\beta_0+\beta_1x_{i1}+\beta_2x_{i2}+\cdots+\beta_px_{ip}+\varepsilon_i, \qquad i=1,\dots,n, \end{equation} where the errors satisfy ε1,…,εnare independent,𝔼[εi]=0,Var⁡(εi)=σ2,\begin{equation} \label{eq:assumptions} \varepsilon_1,\dots,\varepsilon_n\ \text{are independent},\qquad \mathbb{E}[\varepsilon_i]=0,\qquad \operatorname{Var}(\varepsilon_i)=\sigma^2, \end{equation} and, for inference, εi∼N(0,σ2)\varepsilon_i\sim N(0,\sigma^2). The quantity 𝔼[yi∣xi1,…,xip]=β0+β1xi1+⋯+βpxip\mathbb{E}[y_i\mid x_{i1},\dots,x_{ip}]=\beta_0+\beta_1x_{i1}+\cdots+\beta_px_{ip} is the regression function. The parameter βj\beta_j is the change in the mean response associated with a one-unit increase in xjx_j holding all other predictors fixed.

    矩阵形式的模型The model in matrix form

    Stacking the nn equations （见公式） gives (y1y2⋮yn)⏟𝒚(n×1)=(1x11x12⋯x1p1x21x22⋯x2p⋮⋮⋮⋱⋮1xn1xn2⋯xnp)⏟𝑿(n×(p+1))(β0β1⋮βp)⏟𝜷((p+1)×1)+(ε1ε2⋮εn)⏟𝜺(n×1).\begin{equation} \label{eq:matrixmodel} \underbrace{\begin{pmatrix}y_1\\y_2\\\vdots\\y_n\end{pmatrix}}_{\bm y\ (n\times1)} = \underbrace{\begin{pmatrix} 1 & x_{11} & x_{12} & \cdots & x_{1p}\\ 1 & x_{21} & x_{22} & \cdots & x_{2p}\\ \vdots & \vdots & \vdots & \ddots & \vdots\\ 1 & x_{n1} & x_{n2} & \cdots & x_{np} \end{pmatrix}}_{\bm X\ (n\times(p+1))} \underbrace{\begin{pmatrix}\beta_0\\\beta_1\\\vdots\\\beta_p\end{pmatrix}}_{\bm\beta\ ((p+1)\times1)} + \underbrace{\begin{pmatrix}\varepsilon_1\\\varepsilon_2\\\vdots\\\varepsilon_n\end{pmatrix}}_{\bm\varepsilon\ (n\times1)} . \end{equation} Write k:=p+1k:=p+1 for the number of regression parameters (intercept plus slopes). In compact form, 𝒚=𝑿𝜷+𝜺,𝔼[𝜺]=𝟎,Var⁡(𝜺)=σ2𝑰n,𝜺∼Nn(𝟎,σ2𝑰n)for inference.\bm y=\bm X\bm\beta+\bm\varepsilon,\qquad \mathbb{E}[\bm\varepsilon]=\bm 0,\qquad \operatorname{Var}(\bm\varepsilon)=\sigma^2\bm I_n,\qquad \bm\varepsilon\sim N_n(\bm 0,\sigma^2\bm I_n)\ \text{for inference.} 𝑿\bm X is the design matrix. In the rest of these notes 𝑿\bm X always contains a column of ones, so the model has an intercept; the last part of §6.13 explains what changes if it does not.

    注记

    Remark 1 (Full rank). All of the algebra below assumes rank⁡(𝑿)=k=p+1\operatorname{rank}(\bm X)=k=p+1, i.e. the columns of 𝑿\bm X are linearly independent. This is exactly the condition under which 𝑿⊤𝑿\bm X^{\top}\bm X is invertible. It fails if, for example, one predictor is a linear combination of the others, or if a categorical predictor with rr levels is coded with rr dummy variables while an intercept is also present (the “dummy-variable trap”).

    最小二乘准则与正规方程The least squares criterion and the normal equations

    定义

    Definition 2 (Least squares estimator). The least squares estimator 𝜷̂\hat{\bm\beta} minimises the residual sum of squares SS(𝜷)=(𝒚−𝑿𝜷)⊤(𝒚−𝑿𝜷)=∑i=1n(yi−∑j=0pβjxij)2,xi0:=1.\begin{equation} \label{eq:SS} SS(\bm\beta)=(\bm y-\bm X\bm\beta)^{\top}(\bm y-\bm X\bm\beta) =\sum_{i=1}^{n}\Bigl(y_i-\sum_{j=0}^{p}\beta_jx_{ij}\Bigr)^{2}, \qquad x_{i0}:=1 . \end{equation}

    Theorem 3 (Normal equations). If rank⁡(𝑿)=p+1\operatorname{rank}(\bm X)=p+1 then SS(𝜷)SS(\bm\beta) has the unique minimiser 𝜷̂=(𝑿⊤𝑿)−1𝑿⊤𝒚\begin{equation} \label{eq:betahat} {\ \hat{\bm\beta}=(\bm X^{\top}\bm X)^{-1}\bm X^{\top}\bm y\ } \end{equation} and 𝜷̂\hat{\bm\beta} solves the normal equations 𝑿⊤𝑿𝜷=𝑿⊤𝒚\bm X^{\top}\bm X\,\bm\beta=\bm X^{\top}\bm y.

    证明

    Proof. Expand the quadratic form: SS(𝜷)=𝒚⊤𝒚−2𝜷⊤𝑿⊤𝒚+𝜷⊤𝑿⊤𝑿𝜷.\begin{equation} \label{eq:expand} SS(\bm\beta) =\bm y^{\top}\bm y-2\bm\beta^{\top}\bm X^{\top}\bm y+\bm\beta^{\top}\bm X^{\top}\bm X\bm\beta . \end{equation} We need two matrix-calculus facts, both obtained by writing the expression out in components and differentiating with respect to each βj\beta_j: ∂(𝒂⊤𝜷)∂𝜷=𝒂,∂(𝜷⊤𝑨𝜷)∂𝜷=(𝑨+𝑨⊤)𝜷.\frac{\partial(\bm a^{\top}\bm\beta)}{\partial\bm\beta}=\bm a, \qquad \frac{\partial(\bm\beta^{\top}\bm A\bm\beta)}{\partial\bm\beta} =(\bm A+\bm A^{\top})\bm\beta . (For the second: 𝜷⊤𝑨𝜷=∑r,sArsβrβs\bm\beta^{\top}\bm A\bm\beta=\sum_{r,s}A_{rs}\beta_r\beta_s, and ∂/∂βj\partial/\partial\beta_j of that is ∑sAjsβs+∑rArjβr\sum_s A_{js}\beta_s+\sum_r A_{rj}\beta_r, which is the jj-th entry of (𝑨+𝑨⊤)𝜷(\bm A+\bm A^{\top})\bm\beta.) Applying these to （见公式） with 𝑨=𝑿⊤𝑿\bm A=\bm X^{\top}\bm X symmetric gives the gradient ∂SS(𝜷)∂𝜷=−2𝑿⊤𝒚+2𝑿⊤𝑿𝜷=−2𝑿⊤(𝒚−𝑿𝜷).\begin{equation} \label{eq:gradient} \frac{\partial SS(\bm\beta)}{\partial\bm\beta} =-2\bm X^{\top}\bm y+2\bm X^{\top}\bm X\bm\beta =-2\bm X^{\top}(\bm y-\bm X\bm\beta). \end{equation} Setting this to zero gives the normal equations 𝑿⊤𝑿𝜷=𝑿⊤𝒚\bm X^{\top}\bm X\bm\beta=\bm X^{\top}\bm y. Because rank⁡(𝑿)=p+1\operatorname{rank}(\bm X)=p+1, 𝑿⊤𝑿\bm X^{\top}\bm X is positive definite and hence invertible, so the unique stationary point is （见公式）. Finally, the Hessian ∂2SS(𝜷)∂𝜷∂𝜷⊤=2𝑿⊤𝑿\frac{\partial^{2}SS(\bm\beta)}{\partial\bm\beta\,\partial\bm\beta^{\top}} =2\bm X^{\top}\bm X is positive definite, so （见公式） is a minimum (indeed the unique global minimum, since SSSS is a strictly convex quadratic). ◻

    拟合值、残差与帽子矩阵Fitted values, residuals and the hat matrix

    定义

    Definition 5 (Fitted values, residuals, hat matrix). 𝒚̂:=𝑿𝜷̂,𝒆:=𝒚−𝒚̂,𝑯:=𝑿(𝑿⊤𝑿)−1𝑿⊤.\hat{\bm y}:=\bm X\hat{\bm\beta},\qquad \bm e:=\bm y-\hat{\bm y},\qquad \bm H:=\bm X(\bm X^{\top}\bm X)^{-1}\bm X^{\top}. 𝑯\bm H is called the hat matrix because 𝒚̂=𝑯𝒚\hat{\bm y}=\bm H\bm y: it puts the hat on 𝒚\bm y.

    Proposition 6 (Properties of 𝑯\bm H). The matrix 𝑯\bm H is symmetric and idempotent: 𝑯⊤=𝑯\bm H^{\top}=\bm H and 𝑯2=𝑯\bm H^{2}=\bm H. Consequently 𝒚̂=𝑯𝒚,𝒆=(𝑰n−𝑯)𝒚,\hat{\bm y}=\bm H\bm y,\qquad \bm e=(\bm I_n-\bm H)\bm y, and 𝑰n−𝑯\bm I_n-\bm H is also symmetric and idempotent, 𝑯(𝑰n−𝑯)=𝟎\bm H(\bm I_n-\bm H)=\bm 0.     证明

    Proof. Symmetry: 𝑯⊤=(𝑿(𝑿⊤𝑿)−1𝑿⊤)⊤=𝑿((𝑿⊤𝑿)−1)⊤𝑿⊤=𝑯\bm H^{\top}=(\bm X(\bm X^{\top}\bm X)^{-1}\bm X^{\top})^{\top} =\bm X((\bm X^{\top}\bm X)^{-1})^{\top}\bm X^{\top}=\bm H, because 𝑿⊤𝑿\bm X^{\top}\bm X and hence its inverse are symmetric. Idempotence: 𝑯2=𝑿(𝑿⊤𝑿)−1𝑿⊤𝑿(𝑿⊤𝑿)−1⏟𝑰n𝑿⊤=𝑿(𝑿⊤𝑿)−1𝑿⊤=𝑯.\bm H^{2}=\bm X(\bm X^{\top}\bm X)^{-1}\underbrace{\bm X^{\top}\bm X(\bm X^{\top}\bm X)^{-1}}_{\bm I_n}\bm X^{\top} =\bm X(\bm X^{\top}\bm X)^{-1}\bm X^{\top}=\bm H. Then 𝒆=𝒚−𝑿𝜷̂=(𝑰n−𝑯)𝒚\bm e=\bm y-\bm X\hat{\bm\beta}=(\bm I_n-\bm H)\bm y, and (𝑰n−𝑯)2=𝑰n−2𝑯+𝑯2=𝑰n−𝑯(\bm I_n-\bm H)^{2}=\bm I_n-2\bm H+\bm H^{2}=\bm I_n-\bm H.     估计量的均值与协方差Mean and covariance of the least squares estimator

    Theorem 8 (Unbiasedness and covariance). Under 𝒚=𝑿𝜷+𝜺\bm y=\bm X\bm\beta+\bm\varepsilon with 𝔼[𝜺]=𝟎\mathbb{E}[\bm\varepsilon]=\bm 0 and Var⁡(𝜺)=σ2𝑰n\operatorname{Var}(\bm\varepsilon)=\sigma^{2}\bm I_n, 𝔼[𝜷̂]=𝜷,Var⁡(𝜷̂)=σ2(𝑿⊤𝑿)−1.\begin{equation} \label{eq:meanvar} \mathbb{E}[\hat{\bm\beta}]=\bm\beta, \qquad \operatorname{Var}(\hat{\bm\beta})=\sigma^{2}(\bm X^{\top}\bm X)^{-1}. \end{equation} Moreover, if 𝜺∼Nn(𝟎,σ2𝑰n)\bm\varepsilon\sim N_n(\bm 0,\sigma^{2}\bm I_n) then 𝜷̂∼Np+1(𝜷,σ2(𝑿⊤𝑿)−1).\begin{equation} \label{eq:betanormal} \hat{\bm\beta}\sim N_{p+1}\bigl(\bm\beta,\ \sigma^{2}(\bm X^{\top}\bm X)^{-1}\bigr). \end{equation}

    证明

    ANOVA 表的完整推导The ANOVA table: derivations

    本节导读：本章的核心。按四个逻辑步骤拆开：（1）纯代数的分解 TSS = RegSS + RSS；（2）MSE 是 σ² 的无偏估计；（3）正态性下 RSS 与 RegSS 的 χ² 分布及相互独立；（4）由此得到整体 F 检验。要点是让学生清楚每一步分别用到了什么假设。

    This is the core of the chapter. We build the ANOVA table in four logically separate steps, because it is important to see what is pure algebra and what needs the normal model:

    1. Algebra (§3.1): the identity TSS=RegSS+RSS\mathrm{TSS}=\mathrm{Reg\,SS}+\mathrm{RSS}, which holds for the least squares fit regardless of any distributional assumption.

    2. Unbiasedness (§3.2): 𝔼[MSE]=σ2\mathbb{E}[\mathrm{MSE}]=\sigma^{2}, which also needs only （见公式） and not normality.

    3. Distributions (§3.3): under normality, RSS/σ2∼χn−k2\mathrm{RSS}/\sigma^{2}\sim\chi^{2}_{n-k}, RegSS/σ2∼χp2\mathrm{Reg\,SS}/\sigma^{2}\sim\chi^{2}_{p} under H0H_0, and the two are independent.

    4. The test (§3.4): the ratio therefore has an FF distribution, which is the ANOVA FF-test.

    第 1 步：ANOVA 恒等式Step 1: the ANOVA identity T S S = R e g S S + R S S

    影响分析与 Cook 距离Influence and Cook’s distance

    An observation is influential if removing it changes the fitted coefficients substantially. Let ŷ(i)\hat y_{(i)} denote the fitted values from the model fitted without observation ii.

    Outlier, leverage and influence are three different things, and the worked example illustrates all three combinations:

    • an outlier with small leverage: a large residual at an ordinary 𝒙\bm x; it moves the fit little but inflates MSE\mathrm{MSE};

    • a high-leverage point that is not an outlier: an unusual 𝒙\bm x that lies close to the fitted surface; it does not change the fit much;

    • an influential point: usually an outlier with high leverage, which both moves the surface and inflates MSE\mathrm{MSE}.

    Unusual observations should not be deleted automatically. First ask why they are unusual (recording error, a missing predictor, a genuinely different sub-population); then decide whether to correct the data, refit without them as a sensitivity check, extend the model, or use a robust method.

    去掉截距后有哪些变化What changes without an intercept

    If the model has no intercept, none of the ANOVA table above applies as written. The reason is that 𝟏\bm 1 is no longer a column of 𝑿\bm X, so the fitted values need not sum to the observed ones and （见公式） fails at the step ∑iŷi=∑iyi\sum_i\hat y_i=\sum_iy_i. The “total” sum of squares then becomes the uncorrected ∑iyi2\sum_iy_i^{2} rather than the centred ∑i(yi−y‾)2\sum_i(y_i-\bar y)^{2}, and the regression sum of squares is no longer a sum of squared deviations from a mean. For the worked example, the no-intercept fit has RSS=32.296350\mathrm{RSS}=32.296350, which exceeds ∑i(yi−y‾)2=25.54875\sum_i(y_i-\bar y)^{2}=25.54875: the uncorrected total is ∑iyi2=859.91\sum_iy_i^{2}=859.91, and the printed R2R^{2} is not comparable with the with-intercept value. This is why statistical software warns about models without an intercept and why the intercept should be retained unless there is a strong substantive reason – and a re-derivation.

    Common student errors to anticipate

    1. Using n−2n-2 instead of n−p−1n-p-1. Ask: how many parameters did you estimate? The error df is nn minus that number.

    帽子矩阵是什么、为什么叫“帽子矩阵”What 𝑯 is, and why it is called the hat matrix

    With 𝒚=𝑿𝜷+𝜺\bm y=\bm X\bm\beta+\bm\varepsilon and 𝜷̂=(𝑿⊤𝑿)−1𝑿⊤𝒚\hat{\bm\beta}=(\bm X^{\top}\bm X)^{-1}\bm X^{\top}\bm y, 𝒚̂=𝑿𝜷̂=𝑿(𝑿⊤𝑿)−1𝑿⊤𝒚=𝑯𝒚,𝑯=𝑿(𝑿⊤𝑿)−1𝑿⊤\begin{equation} \label{eq:H} \hat{\bm y}=\bm X\hat{\bm\beta}=\bm X(\bm X^{\top}\bm X)^{-1}\bm X^{\top}\bm y =\bm H\bm y, \qquad {\ \bm H=\bm X(\bm X^{\top}\bm X)^{-1}\bm X^{\top}\ } \end{equation} so 𝑯\bm H puts the hat on 𝒚\bm y: it maps the observed responses to the fitted values. It is an n×nn\times n matrix (square, and in general not of full rank), built entirely from the design matrix — 𝒚\bm y does not appear in it at all.

    注记

    Remark 41 (The geometric content). 𝑯\bm H is the matrix of the orthogonal projection of ℝn\mathbb{R}^{n} onto the column space of 𝑿\bm X, 𝒞(𝑿)\mathcal{C}(\bm X); and 𝑰n−𝑯\bm I_n-\bm H is the projection onto the orthogonal complement 𝒞(𝑿)⟂\mathcal{C}(\bm X)^{\perp}. So 𝒚̂=𝑯𝒚∈𝒞(𝑿),𝒆=(𝑰n−𝑯)𝒚∈𝒞(𝑿)⟂,\hat{\bm y}=\bm H\bm y\in\mathcal{C}(\bm X),\qquad \bm e=(\bm I_n-\bm H)\bm y\in\mathcal{C}(\bm X)^{\perp}, and 𝒚̂⊤𝒆=0\hat{\bm y}^{\top}\bm e=0. This single fact is the source of TSS=RegSS+RSS\mathrm{TSS}=\mathrm{Reg\,SS}+\mathrm{RSS} (the Chapter 3 ANOVA identity) and of the residual orthogonality 𝑿⊤𝒆=𝟎\bm X^{\top}\bm e=\bm 0.

    注记

    Remark 42 (How to read the entries). Row ii of 𝑯\bm H gives the weights with which the observations enter the ii-th fitted value: ŷi=∑j=1nhijyj=hi1y1+⋯+hiiyi+⋯+hinyn.\hat y_i=\sum_{j=1}^{n}h_{ij}y_j=h_{i1}y_1+\cdots+h_{ii}y_i+\cdots+h_{in}y_n .     命题

    Proposition 51 (Sums of squares as quadratic forms). 𝒚̂=𝑯𝒚,𝒆=(𝑰n−𝑯)𝒚,RegSS=𝒚⊤(𝑯−1n𝟏𝟏⊤)𝒚,RSS=𝒚⊤(𝑰n−𝑯)𝒚.\hat{\bm y}=\bm H\bm y,\qquad \bm e=(\bm I_n-\bm H)\bm y,\qquad \mathrm{Reg\,SS}=\bm y^{\top}\Bigl(\bm H-\tfrac1n\bm 1\bm 1^{\top}\Bigr)\bm y,\qquad \mathrm{RSS}=\bm y^{\top}(\bm I_n-\bm H)\bm y. Under 𝜺∼Nn(𝟎,σ2𝑰n)\bm\varepsilon\sim N_n(\bm 0,\sigma^{2}\bm I_n), RSS/σ2∼χn−k2\mathrm{RSS}/\sigma^{2}\sim\chi^{2}_{n-k}; and under H0:β1=⋯=βp=0H_0:\beta_1=\cdots=\beta_p=0, RegSS/σ2∼χp2\mathrm{Reg\,SS}/\sigma^{2}\sim\chi^{2}_{p} independently of RSS\mathrm{RSS}, hence F=MSR/MSE∼Fp,n−kF=\mathrm{MSR}/\mathrm{MSE}\sim F_{p,n-k}.

    证明

    Why the ranks are what they are. yhat <- H %*% y
