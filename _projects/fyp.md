At Xi'an Jiaotong-Liverpool University, each final-year student gets an individual topic on which he/she works for two semesters in close collaboration with a personal supervisor. Within each project, each student works on a (small) research topic and produces a thesis and a presentation about the topic and his/her findings. In some projects the focus may be on literature studies and on the combination of information in scientific papers; in others, the evaluation of statistics or writing small computer programs may be required.
